EMI guide for Indian borrowers

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1). P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Home loan EMI

Home loans have the longest terms, often 15 to 30 years, and the lowest rates. A small rate cut or a short term can save lakhs in interest. Many borrowers also claim tax benefits on principal and interest, so check current rules.

Car loan EMI

Car loans run 3 to 7 years. A bigger down payment lowers your EMI and the interest you pay, because the car loses value faster than a house.

Personal loan EMI

Personal loans are unsecured, so rates are higher, often 10% to 20%. Keep the term short and borrow only what you need.

Does prepayment help?

Yes. Extra payments go straight to principal, which cuts interest and ends the loan earlier. Use the prepayment box above to see how much you save.

Which is cheaper, a long or a short term?

A long term gives a lower EMI but costs more in total interest. Use the Compare tab to see the exact difference.
